02 / BEHIND THE SOUND

A DJ with its
source code showing.

Every record has a plan. Every performance has room to move.

01

Compose a record.

A seed chooses the key, progression, form and coordinated instruments. Phrases repeat and answer each other, giving each record an identity.

02

Mix the next one.

Two decks share a beat grid. Eight- or sixteen-bar handovers move the tempo and clear old pitched parts before the new key arrives.

03

Make it your set.

Steer Chaos, Density, Mutation and Tension. Hold a record, request a fill, or start a filter, echo, wash or rhythmic stutter.
